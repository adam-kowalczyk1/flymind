# F1 Stage 0 protocol r1 - adversarial red-team review (round 1)

Scope: PROTOCOL.md, protocol.json, SEED_REGISTRY.md (f1_active_sensing/) checked against encoder.py, alif.py, visual_path.py, loop.py, so101.py,
sensory.py, block_shuffle.py, side_shuffle.py, program_v5v41/FINAL_REPORT.md. Read-only. No MuJoCo run. One small numpy-only script
(scratchpad/rt_jit.py) was run for the jitter statistics; its output is quoted below. No file inside so101_fly_project was touched.
Note: only the three protocol files exist in the F1 directory; no runner, analysis or test code was available, so "tested" claims in the protocol are unverified.

## Verdict: FAIL as written (r1). Needs a revision r2 before the pilot. The fixes are cheap (no new physics, mostly ladder/control/statistics design).

## CRITICAL

### C1. The 5-point rho ladder x steep threshold makes the primary metric an aliasing measure of "where the step falls", not a benefit; S3/S4/S6 inherit it
Evidence: designer's probe: 12*g = 6.02 / 5.05 / 4.23 mV at rho 0.26 / 0.30 / 0.34 (az 8 deg), i.e. about 0.45 mV per 0.02 m rung; DNa02 goes 0 -> 11.5 Hz inside <1 mV;
the effective noise is 0.3 mV on the membrane (alif.py:62-66; sigma*sqrt(dt/2tau_m) = 0.32 mV) and pixel noise adds a few hundredths of a mV
(ball with k=1 has hundreds of px, sigma_c ~ 0.024/px after the 3x3 blur, encoder.py:68-72). Between-network thresholds differ by only 0.10-0.17 mV
(FINAL_REPORT section C: I_th10 5.99 vs 6.09, I_th30 6.49 vs 6.66) = 0.2-0.4 rung. The scene itself has a left/right drive asymmetry of 0.37 mV at +/-10 deg
(g 0.4415 vs 0.4105, x12) = 0.8 rung. So at most 1-2 of 5 levels are ever in the transition; the rest are floor (rho >= 0.30 stationary) or ceiling.
Per-unit outcomes are near-binary and nearly identical across units (the only randomness is 0.3 mV membrane noise and sub-mV pixel noise).
Consequences:
- Ladder-average BSC is the count of rungs crossed / 5 (quantum 0.1 per side, 0.2 per rung). DATA-vs-B1 (S3, S4) differences of 0.1-0.17 mV, and DATA-vs-TH (S6),
  turn on whether a step happens to sit just left or right of a rung. The sign and size of S3/S4/S6 are then arbitrary properties of the ladder, not of the mechanism.
- Bootstrap CIs over units will often be degenerate (zero width: all units agree) so "CI95 > 0" and "CI90 inside +/-0.05" both come out trivially true. Equivalence at
  floor/floor or ceiling/ceiling (Delta = 0 = 0) will be declared "equal" (F-b, F-c) although nothing was measurable.
- 3 of 5 floor levels dilute Delta1 and make SESOI 0.05 meaningless (0.05 = a quarter of a rung).
Fix: (a) replace the 5-rung ladder by a fine drive-matched grid (e.g. rho 0.24-0.40 at 0.005 m, or define levels directly in terms of stationary ipsi drive/threshold-relative
mV and map to rho); rendering is cheap because clean frames are unit-independent (cache them; per unit only noise differs). (b) Primary = threshold shift (rho_50 or mV-equivalent)
estimated by logistic/probit fit on the fine grid, or area under BSC over a grid wide enough that stationary BSC crosses 0.5 for every network; the user range 0.26-0.34 can remain the
reporting range. (c) Pre-register an informativeness gate: a comparison enters the equivalence logic only if the stationary curve of BOTH arms has >= 2 grid points with BSC in (0.1, 0.9);
otherwise report "not measurable", never "equivalent". (d) Cap bootstrap p at 1, use (r+1)/(B+1) (the p = 2*min(...) formula returns 2 or 0 for point-mass bootstrap distributions).

### C2. S5 (P ladder C vs SNN ladder D) and S6 (TH) are not fair SNN-specificity tests
Evidence and reasoning:
- S5 subtracts ladder-average Delta of two systems on two unrelated ladders (7 contrast levels, nonuniform; 5 distance rungs). A ladder-average Delta depends on how many rungs fall in each
  system's transition. P's transition is the encoder c_min step (encoder.py:70, c_min 0.15): with pixel noise sigma 10 and no target, g == 0 exactly (sigma_c ~0.024 << 0.15), and with a target
  any frame with >=1 pixel above c_min gives g > 0 and, because S_L > S_R for az > 0, D > 0 with c = 0 (C3). Designer's own numbers: k 0.25 -> n_px ~28, i.e. P is already at ceiling at k >= 0.25;
  so about 5 of 7 ladder-C levels are ceiling, 1 floor (k 0.15, unmeasured), 1 transition. Delta(P, C) will be ~0 by ladder geometry, so S5 = Delta(DATA) - ~0 > 0 regardless of SNN specificity.
- S6: TH threshold 5.99 mV is the DATA I_th10 (10 Hz point of a STEADY unilateral current, FINAL_REPORT section C). The SNN's actual hit criterion in F1 is D != 0 in 2 s (>= 1 net spike, ~0.5 Hz), which is
  reached at lower drive (rate goes 0 -> 11.5 Hz between 5.05 and 6.02 mV). So TH is systematically less sensitive than the SNN at stationary, its transition sits at a different rung, and the SNN has integration (tau_m 20 ms),
  recurrent LC10a excitation, push-pull inhibition and adaptation (b_a 0.5 mV, tau_a 300 ms, alif.py ALIFParams) that TH lacks; also TH sees both sides' drive without cross-inhibition. Delta(DATA) - Delta(TH) therefore measures operating-point mismatch.
  A "memoryless threshold" is a fair control only if its threshold is placed where the SNN's stationary psychometric function is.
- LIN (linear, no c_min): in expectation the window mean of a linear functional of a symmetrically moving image is unchanged, so Delta(LIN) ~ 0 by construction. It is a useful NULL control (motion adds no information to a
  linear integrator), not an "image-information reference/bound"; call it that.
Fix: (a) Calibrate TH (and a second control, TH + low-pass tau 20 ms + adaptation, i.e. a single-LIF surrogate) on PILOT stationary data so that its stationary BSC curve equals DATA's (pre-registered least-squares rule on the fine grid), then freeze;
report the 5.99 mV version as a labelled variant. (b) For S5 put all systems on the SAME stimulus axis expressed as effective drive (e.g. distance in "threshold units": rho relative to each system's own stationary rho_50, or contrast ladder shifted per system so that
stationary BSC passes through 0.5 at the middle level), and compare threshold shifts (in threshold units), not ladder-average BSC. Give P a ladder C that straddles ITS step (finer around k 0.12-0.30); pre-register the gate from C1(c).
(c) Add DATA on a contrast ladder too, or state that S5 compares different stimulus dimensions and cannot be interpreted as "SNN vs classical at equal operating point".

### C3. FA-matched criterion is vacuous for four of five systems; the SDT apparatus does not do what section 5 says
Evidence: no-target images contain no green; sigma_c = (10/3)*sqrt(1.5)/lum_ratio ~ 0.024-0.03 after 3x3 blur; c_min = 0.15 is ~5-6 sigma, 12 288 px => P(any pixel above) ~ 1e-5/frame. So g = 0, D == 0 for SNN (LC10a silent), P and TH on every no-target trial;
FA = P(|D| > 0) = 0, hence c = 0 for all of them ("c = 0 if already satisfied"). Only LIN has a non-degenerate c. Therefore: (i) "FA matched at 0.05" matches nothing - the five systems run at c = 0 with FA = 0 and differ in built-in sensitivity floors (hair-trigger c_min step for P/TH, 6 mV LC10a threshold for SNN);
(ii) LIN is the only system that pays an FA price, so LIN is not comparable; (iii) d'_det is infinite/clipped for four systems (AUC clip 1/(2n) -> d' ~ 3.97 at n = 200) and carries no information; (iv) "no-target bias" and "false-alarm" never get exercised; there is no nuisance in the no-target scene (the repo already supports a
distractor: SceneCfg.distractor_r / reset(distractor_pos)). In 0D (pixel sigma 20, c noise 0.049, c_min = 3.06 sigma, ~14 noise px/frame) P/TH suddenly acquire FA > 0 - so 0D is not comparable to the main design for the non-neural systems.
Fix: add a pre-registered nuisance to the no-target trials (low-contrast green distractor sphere at |az| 25-35 deg, or a faint distractor of random side) so that FA is a real quantity, and report actual FA for every system; state that at FA = 0 comparison is of hit rate at the minimum criterion; for LIN use the same nuisance. Report FA for all systems as a row in every table. Alternatively drop "FA-matched" wording and call it "criterion = 0 unless no-target D has support".

### C4. Jitter controls (S1, S2) are under-sampled, mis-windowed, and can have a DC offset; the RMS-matched jitter is unfair to the sinusoid under a threshold
Evidence (rt_jit.py, 2000 simulated realisations of the protocol's J_vel recipe, A = 2 deg, rescaled to RMS A/sqrt2 over the 3 s trial, 100 Hz frames):
- Only 4 realisations per condition (section 3), assigned u mod 4 - so S1/S2 compare the sinusoid against four specific waveforms. With a steep threshold the response depends on peak excursions: for white jitter at RMS A/sqrt2 the window max|theta|/A has median 2.07 (5-95% 1.72-2.58, sd 0.27 across realisations) versus exactly 1.0 for the sinusoid. A single realisation changes the effective peak by ~13 % = more than one rung. Inference is conditional on four draws, not on "jitter".
- J_vel: RMS is rescaled over the whole trial (frames 0-299) but analysed in frames 100-299; window RMS/target 5-95%: 0.63-1.19 at f 0.5 Hz, 0.72-1.17 at 1 Hz. The sinusoid, by contrast, is exact over the window only (200 frames = whole cycles).
- J_vel has a DC component: median |window mean|/RMS = 0.46 (0.5 Hz), 0.31 (1 Hz), 0.22 (2 Hz), 0.15 (4 Hz). A 0.5-0.9 deg standing offset is a stationary retinal shift of the same size as the effect (and ~1/3-1/2 rung in drive); the sinusoid has zero mean by construction.
- The advertised test "realised RMS velocity within +/-20 %" would FAIL for 44 % of realisations at 0.5 Hz, 28 % at 1 Hz, 13 % at 2 Hz, 4 % at 4 Hz. The protocol does not say what happens (reject? resample?).
- RMS-matching is the wrong match for a threshold system: same RMS but heavy tails (Gaussian) vs bounded sinusoid => white jitter reaches 2x the peak and will beat the sinusoid at a threshold for trivial amplitude-distribution reasons; "no sinusoid specificity" (F-b) would then be a statement about tails.
Fix: (a) >= 32 jitter realisations per condition (draw per unit from the unit seed, or realisation index = floor(j / n_inst) mod 32) and report between-realisation SD; (b) match RMS theta AND zero mean AND RMS theta-dot in the ANALYSIS WINDOW (frames 100-299), by rejection sampling inside the seeded stream (fixed max attempts, recorded) or by a Fourier-domain construction with prescribed spectrum and random phases; (c) add two controls that isolate waveform factors: (i) time-shuffled sinusoid (same samples, same amplitude distribution, destroyed temporal order) and (ii) random-phase narrowband noise at f (same bandwidth-limited spectrum around f, exact window RMS) plus a peak-matched jitter (clipped/arcsine amplitude distribution); (d) pre-register that S1/S2 compare against the whole control family, not a single draw.

## MAJOR

### M1. Unit counterbalancing is broken for the 10-instance confirm design (u mod 10 with u mod 4)
Evidence: instance = u mod n_inst, phase = u mod 4, jitter realisation = u mod 4. rt_jit.py: with n_inst = 10 only 20 of 40 (instance, phase) pairs occur; instance 0 only ever sees phases {0, pi}, instance 1 only {pi/2, 3pi/2}. Parity of phase is perfectly confounded with the parity of the B1/SWS instance. The same happens for J realisation. Also n (clamped 200-600, e.g. 237) need not be a multiple of 4 or 40.
Fix: j = u - u0; instance = j mod n_inst; phase = (j // n_inst) mod 4; jitter realisation = (j // n_inst) mod R; require n multiple of lcm(n_inst, 4, R) (round up); apply the same index map to DATA.

### M2. Generalisation: the experiment is a noise-replicate study of a fixed, deterministic stimulus set
Units differ only in ALIF noise (0.3 mV) and sub-mV pixel noise; geometry, ladder, 4 phases and 4 jitter draws are fixed. The bootstrap CI therefore expresses noise sampling only. n = 200-600 buys nothing against stimulus-selection uncertainty (which rung, which az, which draw). R2 will almost surely return SD ~ 0 and n = 200 by clamp; "power" is meaningless.
Fix: sample stimuli per unit (az0 ~ U[8,12] deg, rho offset ~ U[-0.005, 0.005] m, jitter fresh per unit, random contrast/gain nuisance of a few percent in the encoder illumination, e.g. global image gain 0.9-1.1 before noise), so that the unit is the right resampling unit; keep the fixed ladder as a reporting grid. Declare conclusions conditional on the stimulus family otherwise.

### M3. Instance handling for S3/S4 (DATA is 1 network, B1/SWS ~ one network each)
FINAL_REPORT section A: "the SWS family behaves almost like one network"; B1 likewise. Units are resampled, instances are not (instance is a function of u), so the DATA-vs-shuffle CI is a noise-replicate CI, and with 10 instances the shuffle-class variance is partly confounded (M1). The correct statement is "DATA vs these 10 instances"; a DATA-vs-class test needs the rank of DATA among instances (permutation/rank) or a two-level bootstrap (instances, then units within instance). Also state explicitly that S3/S4 inherit C1 (threshold offsets of 0.1-0.17 mV).
Fix: report per-instance Delta and the rank of DATA among 10+10 instances; use nested bootstrap (resample instances, then units within) for the interaction; drop "wiring-specific" language unless the rank is extreme.

### M4. R1 selection and R2 sample-size rules
- R1: argmax over 16 cells of the pooled mean of DATA/B1/SWS: pooled over networks it can select a cell good for the shuffles and null for DATA (DATA is one network; if DATA's step lies between rungs, Delta(DATA) can be 0 while B1/SWS give 0.2). The H1 test is then run on DATA at a cell chosen for other networks. It is not biased towards positive H1 (fresh confirm units remove unit-level winner's curse), but it is not aimed at H1 either. Because the effect is deterministic (C1/M2), the pilot argmax is basically the max-amplitude cell; "ties within 0.01 -> smaller A" will pick A = 0.5 deg, f = 0.5 Hz (the weakest cell) if all pilot cells are floor/ceiling-tied, making H1 a test of a cell chosen to fail - mechanical but uninformative. H1 is also a max-over-grid claim ("some sinusoid cell helps") and should be worded that way.
- R2: uses alpha 0.05 two-sided, power 90 %, but the S-family is Holm-adjusted (smallest p must pass 0.05/6 -> needs 1.46x n) and TOST needs CI90 half-width < 0.05; SD is taken only for H1 and S3 (not S1, S2, S4-S6, whose SD are larger: differences of differences). SD from 60 pilot units is itself uncertain (SE ~ 9 %), the 1.2 inflation does not cover Holm. n is a clamped constant, so "power reported if cap binds" should be pre-computed for all S.
Fix: choose the cell by DATA-only criterion (or require DATA > 0 as a condition for "H1-eligible" cells and, if none, run the confirm with the pre-specified default A = 4 deg, f = 1 Hz and call it a null check); make the tie rule favour LARGER A (power) or declare "no eligible cell -> abort confirm, report pilot as negative"; compute n for the max over {H1, S1..S6} at Holm alpha (alpha/6) with the pilot SD; add a precision criterion for TOST.

### M5. H1 verdict rule is not exhaustive/exclusive; failure conditions F-e, F-f, F-h mix in undefined thresholds; Stage 1 recommendation conflicts with F-b..F-d
- SUPPORTED/TRIVIAL/NO MEANINGFUL/HARMFUL overlap (CI95 > 0 together with CI90 inside (-0.05, 0.05) is both TRIVIAL and NO MEANINGFUL). SUPPORTED needs only point Delta >= 0.05, not lower CI bound > 0.05.
- F-e ("benefit only at one ladder level and one grid cell") and F-f ("spike counts rise without a BSC/d' gain") have no numeric definition.
- Recommendation to proceed to Stage 1 "only if H1 SUPPORTED and not F-g" would recommend a closed loop even if F-b (jitter equals sinusoid) and F-c (TH equals SNN) fire, i.e. when the benefit is a static-threshold convexity effect.
Fix: precedence order for verdicts; require lower CI bound >= SESOI for "meaningful" or state why not; numeric definitions for F-e/F-f; Stage 1 recommendation conditional on H1 SUPPORTED and not F-b and not F-c and not F-g.

### M6. "Active sensing / moving sensor, stationary target" is not distinguishable from a moving-target simulation here; image shift is not the bearing shift
- Pure rotation about the world vertical through the optical centre changes only cam orientation (so101.py fk and build_mjcf: camera pos fixed in gripper frame), so the ball world position is indeed unchanged. But the pipeline has no efference copy, no proprioceptive input and uses the same rendered image; a moving ball with fixed camera giving the same bearing sequence would produce the same retinal motion of the ball (only the background moves differently). The experiment tests retinal dithering, not "active" sensing. Say so, and add a control that makes the equivalence explicit: moving-target/static-camera render (ball displaced in world to reproduce the same camera-relative bearing series) - expected equal; a difference reveals background/jaw/lighting contributions.
- Image shift != bearing shift: designer's own probe: +2 deg about world vertical shifted the measured bearing by 2.42 deg (x1.21). Reason: camera pitched ~34 deg down; yaw about the vertical = rotation about image-up by theta*cos(pitch) plus image roll theta*sin(pitch); for a ball below the optical axis the column azimuth used by the encoder (encoder.column_azimuth, S_L(az)) shifts by more than theta and the gain depends on the ball's depression (rho, az, i.e. varies across the ladder). Nominal A is therefore NOT the retinal amplitude fed to the encoder. This affects matching only in the sense that "A deg" does not mean A deg of column azimuth. Also the true-bearing check 'az0 - theta exactly' holds for the ground-plane bearing of loop.bearing, not for the encoder's azimuth.
- Gripper/jaw and table/void edges shift in the image with the virtual rotation (a real eye camera rigid to the gripper would not see them move). Encoder normalises c by whole-image mean luminance (encoder.py:66-69), so motion-locked luminance changes modulate g with theta even with no target. Tolerable only if measured.
Fix: record encoder az_centroid and column-azimuth amplitude per condition and report A_retinal; define matching in retinal units or state the 1.21 factor; add the explicit test "with-target vs no-target g, lum modulation under motion" (no-target D == 0 and g_nt == 0 for every frame; lum range under motion reported); add the moving-target control; word the stage as "retinal-shift (dither) benefit".

### M7. Seed-collision check in SEED_REGISTRY.md is invalid
Evidence: the regex `\b91[0-9][ _]?[0-9]{3}[ _]?[0-9]{3}\b` requires 8 digits; the registered seeds are 7-digit (9 101 001 = 9101001). I verified: `echo 9101001 | grep -E` and `"9 101 001"` both give NO MATCH, so the check cannot match even the registered numbers (the same defect is inherited from program_v5v41, whose regex also demands 9 digits). With a corrected pattern (`\b9[ _]?10[1-9][ _]?[0-9]{3}\b`) over flymind/ and bio2/ (*.py/json/md/txt/jsonl) the only non-F1 hit is the float 1.9109375 in results/v5v41/analysis.json (false positive), so the pools are in fact free - but the registry claim "0 hits" is currently unsupported.
Also SeedSequence([9106000, stage, c, r]) and default_rng(u) / default_rng([u,1]) are distinct streams, fine.
Fix: rerun the collision scan with a regex that matches 7-digit numbers (and a positive control that matches 9 101 001), record both outputs in the registry before the pilot.

### M8. Protocol text has no test specification; several required checks are missing or only "tested" in passing
Missing as explicit, machine-checkable pre-registered tests (listed in the task): zero-amplitude sinusoid == stationary (bit-identical frames); mirror stimuli (scene is not mirror-symmetric, so use a synthetic horizontal flip of the image: encoder g_L <-> g_R exactly and D -> -D for a left/right-swapped LC10a mapping; plus swap-sides readout sign test); no-target bias (mean D, per-side counts, with and without motion; D == 0 expected); phase bias (BSC by phase, test against equality); jitter statistics tests with defined tolerances and the rejection rule (C4); target world position unchanged (ball body pos before/after, hash); renderer pose == recorded pose (cam_xmat vs recorded matrix after mj_camlight - note update_scene uses d.cam_xmat, which is stale unless camlight/forward is called after changing cam_quat); baseline network unmodified (W_data sha256 before/after; the protocol mentions this only for DATA); determinism (same unit twice bit-identical, plus serial vs parallel); readout sign (left target gives D > 0 in DATA; right gives D < 0). Also: PROTOCOL.md (the decision rules) is not hash-locked; only protocol.json is checked by runner.py; the analysis script and the confirm-analysis code must be written and hashed BEFORE the confirm run (cf. V6.1 provenance error).
Missing measure: decision latency (user asked for latency in the SDT set; only first-spike latency and a fixed 1-3 s window are specified). Add time-to-criterion of cumulative D (and of cumulative P/TH) from t = 0.
Fix: add a section "Pre-run tests (blocking)" with IDs, tolerances and a manifest of results; hash PROTOCOL.md + protocol.json + analysis.py + tests together.

## MINOR

- m1. Protocol writes `loop.place_ball(az0, rho)`; the actual function is `place_ball(sim, az0, rho)` with az0 in RADIANS and z = sim.cfg.ball_r (loop.py:77-80). For the 0D small ball (r 1.8 cm) the centre z must be the new radius, not cfg.ball_r; also geom_size AND geom_rbound should be updated in the in-memory model for rendering (culling). State and test.
- m2. CRN (checked): alif.py:62-66 draws `self.rng.standard_normal(self.N)` on every step whenever noise != 0, independent of input; N equal for DATA/B1/SWS (shuffles preserve neurons). So the noise sequence is identical across networks and conditions - valid. Caveats: (i) identical noise across L/R/rho/condition trials in a unit makes all hit decisions of a unit strongly dependent; correct for unit bootstrap, but then "one no-target trial per condition" is paired with the target trials (same pixel noise array), so FA and hits are not independent samples; fine as marginals, wrong if anyone computes pooled ROC variance over trials; (ii) pixel noise array [300,96,128,3] float or uint8: draw once per unit, but state its dtype and that rounding/clipping occurs after adding to the uint8 image; (iii) shared noise means paired differences are nearly noise-free and ordering effects (carry-over) do not exist because ALIF is reset per trial (reset() in __init__ - confirm that a fresh ALIF per trial is built, otherwise state carries over).
- m3. Window-mean P: p is a first-order low-pass of (gL - gR) followed by a window mean => equals the window mean of (gL - gR) up to a start transient (a = exp(-0.1) at 100 Hz, tau 0.1 s, window starts at 1 s = 10 tau). The P "dynamics" are inert; say so.
- m4. The SNN readout counts a 2-neuron difference; D == 0 ties (both silent or both firing equally) are scored "none" = wrong, consistent with the BSC definition, but near the midline (0D az 6 deg, A 4 deg: bearing 2-10 deg) both DNa02 can fire and D ~ 0 - direction information is lost at the readout; F-h covers it only partly. Report the signed D distribution and the fraction of D == 0 among hits.
- m5. d'_LR = sqrt2*Phi^-1(AUC) assumes equal-variance Gaussians; with near-deterministic counts it saturates at the AUC clip (d' ~ 3.97 at n = 200 and depends on n). Do not compare d' across different n (pilot vs confirm). MI plug-in is upward biased at small n; label as descriptive.
- m6. TOST at CI90 is applied per test; the F-b/F-c "or" rules are intersection-union in the wrong direction (either equivalence triggers failure) with no multiplicity control; state that failure conditions are descriptive, not error-controlled.
- m7. 0D uses n = 200 fixed and only DATA + B1 (no SWS, no P/TH/LIN, no jitter controls); F-g "benefit (CI95 > 0) in fewer than 3 of 4 variants" has no benefit-over-control content. With pixel sigma 20 the non-neural systems have FA > 0 (C3).
- m8. Integrity: new files under flymind/experiments/ will change a snapshot of "flymind/"; define the F1 pre-snapshot excluding this directory and the 377-file protected list explicitly; the run opens outputs with mode "x" (good); state that bio2 is never imported by runner/analysis/tests (grep check), and that `python -I -B` is used for the workers too (spawn).
- m9. Camera roll of 2.3 deg and directional light make the scene non-mirror; the protocol correctly reports rather than corrects it, but BSC = (hit_L + hit_R)/2 hides it: report hit_L and hit_R separately as primary descriptive rows (and a side-balanced ladder shifted by the measured asymmetry as a robustness).
- m10. pilot rule R1 uses ladder-D-average Delta, so cells on which DATA's step lies at a different rung than shuffles are weighted by the same C1 aliasing; fixed by C1.

## Direct answers to the question list
1. Can H1/S1-S6 be distinguished given the sharp threshold? Not as designed (C1-C4). A static convex threshold alone predicts H1 SUPPORTED, S6 equivalent, S1 worse than jitter; the protocol's calibration of the competing explanations (TH threshold, ladders, jitter match) is not good enough to separate that from SNN dynamics. Ladder-average BSC with 3/5 floor levels is a poor primary. R1 is not biased toward positive H1 for fresh units, but it is not aimed at DATA and the tie rule can select a null cell. FA-matched criterion: vacuous for zero-activity detectors (c = 0, FA = 0 for SNN/P/TH), non-comparable for LIN.
2. S5 not fair (different ladders/floors/ceilings); TH threshold is the 10 Hz point, not the SNN's hit point; LIN is a null/negative control, not an information reference.
3. J_rms/J_vel: formulas for sigma_t are right (continuous-time ratio 2*pi*f; discrete central difference checked to within ~3 % at 4 Hz) but RMS is set over the trial not the window, DC offset not removed, velocity tolerance not achievable for 28-44 % of draws at low f; 4 realisations is far too few; RMS match is unfair to a bounded sinusoid under a threshold.
4. CRN valid (unconditional draw, same N). Hidden correlation: same noise in all trials of a unit makes hits of one unit dependent, handled by unit bootstrap; no-target and target trials are paired. Phase/instance aliasing (M1) is the real hidden confound.
5. Rotation keeps the world position of the ball; it is retinally indistinguishable from a moving target for this pipeline, the encoder shift is ~1.21x the bearing shift and depends on depression; gripper/background shifts are artefacts (M6).
6. Statistics: bootstrap degenerate near floor/ceiling; p formula can return 0 or 2; criteria re-estimated per resample is right for LIN and moot for the rest; R2 ignores Holm and S1-S6 SD; SESOI 0.05 is a quarter-rung; TOST at floor/floor gives spurious "equal" (C1).
7. Integrity: no protected file is modified by design; seed collision scan is invalid (M7) though the pools are in fact free; PROTOCOL.md not hash-locked; analysis scripts must be frozen before confirm (M8).
8. Missing: decision latency; explicit blocking tests for zero amplitude, mirror (use synthetic flip), no-target bias, phase bias, jitter statistics with a defined rule, renderer pose, target position, readout sign (M8); moving-target control (M6).
