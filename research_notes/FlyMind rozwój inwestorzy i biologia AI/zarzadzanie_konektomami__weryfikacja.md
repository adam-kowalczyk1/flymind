## A. Corrections the report writer MUST apply (top)

1. **FlyWire "latest" snapshot.** The notes say sources disagree and "guidelines page says 630". WRONG. flywire.ai/guidelines (opened today) says the latest public release is version **783**, snapshot of Oct 2023. Zenodo record 10676866 (publication date 2024-06-02) also says 783. Drop the "630 vs 783 disagree" claim; if the manifest argument needs a version-drift example, use FlyWire 630 vs 783 as historical releases, not as a current source conflict (REPORTED, not checked).
2. **FlyWire licence conflict.** The notes state "CC BY-NC 4.0" as fact. The FlyWire guidelines page does say CC BY-NC 4.0 (VERIFIED), but the Zenodo record's metadata says `cc-by-4.0` (VERIFIED via API). Write: "licence stated inconsistently (guidelines: CC BY-NC 4.0; Zenodo: CC BY 4.0) — check before any commercial use". The notes' line "MaleCNS licence not checked" stays true; do not import the "MaleCNS CC-BY" claim from biologia_ai.md without checking it.
3. **Lacuna "control suite" hypothesis is not a "published methodological worry".** Section 2 Inferences says the control-first harness "answers a published methodological worry (Lacuna hypothesis)". OVERCLAIM. The hypothesis (96cf83b9e458c5c2, generated 2026-10-04) is an AI-generated proposal, and it is about **activity-transfer / activation-patching** controls (sham, dose-matched, host variants, purity) plus neuron-model swaps, not about **wiring shuffles/mirrors** that FlyMind runs. It also cites an existing paper that already uses random rewiring (1%, 20%) as a control on the whole connectome ("Network structure governs Drosophila brain functionality"). So: shuffle controls on connectomes are not new, and the Lacuna hypothesis only loosely motivates a FlyMind harness. Reword to "a related AI-generated proposal, not evidence of demand".
4. **"Vision loop specific, grasp not" (concept 1 rationale, section 3).** OVERCLAIM. Project notes (`nasze_wyniki.md` line 48) record that a strength-preserving shuffle gave 10/10 on the vision loop in a preliminary version, and that the merit lies largely in left/right lateralisation, not detailed topology. Strength-matching of the 40-seed vision shuffles is NOT VERIFIED. Say: "on the vision loop the data net beat plain shuffles (38/40 vs 3/40) but strength-matched shuffles were not run at n=40; only the grasp has strength-matched controls and there wiring gives no advantage". Also V5 is a loop result with an artificial colour encoder (Adam's ruling), and the P-controller is 40/40 and more precise.
5. **Project numbers were labelled REPORTED/"task context"; they are in fact verifiable.** I confirmed in the repo: grasp strength-matched shuffles 101,82,80,102,83,79,82,79,99,4 vs 90 (`flymind/reports/bio_advantage_research_20261110.md` line 92: "data network ranks 4th of 11, median shuffle 82"); vision+touch classical 98 vs net 86 (n=135; `flymind/VISION_MANIPULATION_STAGE.md` lines 15–19, 101–105); median 1.2924 ms (`flymind/results/sensory_manipulation/latency_v1.json`). Upgrade these to VERIFIED with file paths. Caveat: the "79–102" range in the notes drops the outlier 4 (one shuffle scored 4); write "79–102 in nine of ten, one at 4".
6. **Lava licence wording.** "BSD-3 core, some Loihi parts proprietary" is imprecise: the README says core is BSD-3 and Loihi hardware backends are LGPL-2.1; GitHub API shows licence NOASSERTION, archived = true, 743 stars (VERIFIED). Say "LGPL-2.1" instead of "proprietary", and "all Lava repositories archived" (README).
7. **Sandia detail.** "Mentions a unified experiment-description language" is not in the arXiv abstract. Remove or mark UNVERIFIABLE (full text not opened). The abstract does say: 140K neurons, 50M synapses, 12 Loihi 2 chips, "orders of magnitude faster" than conventional simulation, performance advantage grows with sparser activity.
8. **Demand/market language.** Section 4 correctly says demand evidence is weak. Keep it, and do not let the report writer turn "hobby repos + Eon + NeuroBench" into "market need". The neuromorphic "0.3 to 20 bn USD" range is aggregator hype (REPORTED); Eon $3.5M seed is secondary-source only. Never present a revenue number for a harness.
9. **Novelty wording.** "Gaps sit between the tools" and "Gap B Open" are INFERRED from landing pages and a handful of Lacuna queries (Lacuna is ML-centric). Always write "I did not find", never "no one provides".
