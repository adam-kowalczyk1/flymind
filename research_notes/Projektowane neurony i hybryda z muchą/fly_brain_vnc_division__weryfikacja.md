## Most important corrections
1. Relabel: every paper-reported number marked "VERIFIED" (rows 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 11) must be REPORTED (read: full text); VERIFIED only for existence/metadata. Update the label definition in the header.
2. Namiki: "190 DNs" is 190 bilateral PAIRS, "at least" 98 types (row 1).
3. Cheong "sparse direct DN-MN": the "rarely emerges" statement is about takeoff; overall ~80% of MN groups have ≥1% direct DN input and groupwise mean DN input is 7%. The "no global number" gap is closed (row 6). The 13/92 count is of groups (row 5). Cheong is 2026 (rows 2, 4).
4. Braun 85% GNG collaterals is cited from a different study, not Braun's own; the 73% is Braun's own (row 7).
5. Rayshubskiy: DNa02 = transient high-gain, DNa01 = sustained low-gain, now read (row 10).
6. Mamiya: "femoral chordotonal" not in abstract; drop (row 16).
7. Overreach: Braun headless result does not "agree with" the red-team finding (row 19); FlyMind does have FeCO proprioception input at the gripper, so "no proprioception" is too strong (row 20).
8. Not rechecked / UNVERIFIABLE by us: "bioRxiv 52 vs published 53 MNs" aside; Eichler 2024; Marin 2024 content; any Azevedo 2024 supplementary muscle-joint table.
