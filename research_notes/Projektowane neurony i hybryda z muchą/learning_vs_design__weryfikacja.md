## Most important corrections

1. Claim 2: the "Loihi vs full-precision GPU actor" reward table is actually PopSAN on Loihi vs the same PopSAN on GPU. It does not show SNN vs ANN. Do not use it for SNN-vs-ANN comparisons.
2. Claim 6: Huebotter seed counts differ (SNN 10 seeds, non-spiking baseline table 3 seeds). SNN 0.986 vs baseline 0.970 success is a reported near-tie from different seed counts, so "comparable" cannot be tested. Exact 3D numbers are in text tables, not only a figure. Still no classical-controller baseline.
3. Claim 9: "lack of practical training methods" belongs to Bing 2020, not the 2018 survey abstract. The list of learning rules is also not in the 2018 abstract.
4. Claim 16: Dampfhoffer 2023 metadata is VERIFIED by Crossref and is not by "the same group" as Yan et al.
5. Claim 21: 97.51% is now REPORTED (abstract via DOAJ), Gao et al. 2024, doi 10.3390/biomimetics9110650; still no trial count or controls. Claim 25 and 24 can be upgraded in existence as above.
6. Claim 20: the review (Qin et al.) shares an author (Fu) with the original 95.3% result, so it is not an independent confirmation; arena sizes were not found in text.
7. Claims 3 and 19: drop "10 episodes up to 1000 steps" and "about 100 contributors" or mark UNVERIFIABLE.
8. Claim 4 add-on: the 140x energy figure is dynamic power only; Loihi was slower per inference than TX2/CPU/GPU in the same table (confirmed).

Everything else sampled (claims 1, 5, 7, 8, 10-15, 17, 18, 22) is CONFIRMED at the level of abstract/metadata.
