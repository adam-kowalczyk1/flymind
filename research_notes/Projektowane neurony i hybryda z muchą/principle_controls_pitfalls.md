## 4. How a hybrid fits or breaks the rule of 3 Nov: where translation ends and deciding begins

### Takeaway
The rule (owner, 3 Nov: not an algorithm but the fly should steer, even at a quality cost; inputs only via natural senses) is violated not by the mere presence of designed neurons but by what they do. A designed part is "translation" only if it is a fixed, memoryless, monotone, one-to-one map from identified fly outputs to joints, defined before testing, with no selection, gating, goal-holding or own state; anything else is deciding. By that test, architecture A (fly decides at DN level, fixed designed layer maps to joints) fits only in its strictest form, E (real fly VNC from data) fits best, C is borderline, and B and D break the rule. Our own current system already shows the ambiguity: the input encoder (colour blob to LC10a current) was ruled artificial stimulation on 12 Nov, and the red team showed a trivial left-to-left relay gives 20/20, i.e. the "fly" part may be lateralization, not decision.

### Inferences
**Operational criteria (HYPOTHESIS, to be fixed by the owner before any build).** A designed element D between fly outputs F and joints J is TRANSLATION only if all hold:
1. T1 Frozen before testing: parameters set by documented rule or one calibration run on a separate dev set, then hashed; no tuning on test scenarios.
2. T2 Memoryless or fixed linear dynamics: output at t depends on F(t-tau..t) through a fixed filter, no latching, no integrator that carries a goal or a mode across trials.
3. T3 Monotone and one-to-one: each joint command is a monotone function of a stated subset of identified fly outputs (e.g. DNa02 L minus R to base pan rate); no cross-joint lookup tables learned from task data.
4. T4 No selection among alternatives: D never picks which of several fly outputs, targets or actions to follow; no winner-take-all, no argmax, no if/else on task state.
5. T5 No gating: D has no input from sensors or task state that multiplies or switches the fly signal (only constant gain, rate limits and safety clipping).
6. T6 No own state that alters which action occurs (counters, timers, memory, learned weights during run).
7. T7 Silence test: with the fly part silenced or replaced by noise, D alone yields no task-appropriate behaviour (success at chance). If D plus constant or trivial input reaches the task, D decides.
8. T8 Information accounting: fraction of task-relevant information (target side, timing, grip level) that D could have obtained on its own inputs equals zero; all of it must enter via F.
Failing T4-T7 = DECIDING. Safety clipping and joint limits are allowed as "safety only" (already allowed by owner: classical code for safety/baseline).
**Also the input side:** the encoder E (sense to fly) is translation only if natural-sense-like (spatial, per-photoreceptor/ per-cell-type), not a hand-chosen current into a hand-chosen neuron class; the 12 Nov ruling puts the colour-blob-to-LC10a encoder on the wrong side (owner decision, REPORTED in task context). Any hybrid has two interfaces (E and D); the rule must be checked at both.
**Classification of candidate architectures (INFERRED, each judged vs the rule):**
- A: fly decides at DN level, designed spinal-like layer maps DN to 6 joints. FITS only if D satisfies T1-T8. Risk: with 6 joints and only 1-2 identified DN pairs (DNa02 for steering; no identified DN for shoulder/elbow/wrist), D must invent the other joint commands, which means it decides (violates T3/T4). A fits for a 1-2 joint subset (base pan, gripper) as today; for 6 joints it silently becomes B or D. Per the 11 Nov/12 Nov local reports, today the net issues only 8.8 % (gripper) and 16.0 % (base) of commands (REPORTED, local report).
- B: designed cortex decides, fly circuits only act as reflexes. BREAKS the rule by definition (selection is in the designed part); fits only as an explicitly named "role B" baseline.
- C: designed neurons inserted inside fly circuits as relays/gain. BORDERLINE-to-BREAKS: a relay with fixed unit gain is translation (and equals our trivial relay baseline, which makes the fly part replaceable); any trained gain, gating or inhibition choosing between pathways is deciding; this is also the hardest to audit because decision is distributed (see degeneracy, topic 5).
- D: fully designed joint-named network (as described on the Founders video, REPORTED: neurons named shoulder_pan_pos_theta1 etc., synapse(pre,post,weight), Training tab). BREAKS the rule: no fly circuit, all weights designed or trained by us/them. Useful only as the comparison arm ("designed network of equal size"), not as a hybrid. The "98 percent of collisions avoided without training" caption is NOT VERIFIED (no trial count or controls in the video as described).
- E: real fly VNC (data, not design) as the translation layer from DN to leg/motor-neuron activity. FITS best (no designed decision); but (i) VNC is a leg/wing controller, mapping to an arm is an engineering analogy only (INFERRED; local report notes no literature solves 6-leg to 5-axis arm), (ii) the read-out from motor neurons to joint torque is a designed layer again (a second D), (iii) data availability in MaleCNS is for topic 3's researcher; here: it moves the interface problem from DN to motor-neuron level but does not remove it.
**Where translation ends:** at the first element that has access to task state or to alternatives. Sharpest practical marker: if replacing D by its linearised, frozen equivalent changes success by more than the noise, D was doing computation beyond translation.
**Local consequence (INFERRED):** the T1 translator and K1 channel of the 12 Nov reports both fail T4/T5 (the translator selects the target; K1 gates eye gain), so under the strict criteria they are role B/C. The owner already marked K1 "boundary"; this note supplies tests, not a ruling.

## 5. Controls that separate "fly decides" from "designed part decides", and typical errors in the field

### Takeaway
No single control suffices; the standard toolkit is causal (lesion/silence, replace-with-null) plus attribution (Shapley-style multi-lesion) plus informational measures, all run with matched random and trivial baselines on held-out scenarios. The field's recurring failure is accepting task success as evidence of biological fidelity: the 2026 "digital sphinx" preprint demonstrates this directly, and our own red-team result (shuffle with preserved strength 10/10, trivial relay 20/20) is the same failure in miniature.

### Cited Findings
- Lesion/inactivation logic with attribution: Shapley value (multi-perturbation Shapley analysis) assigns each element a contribution from performance over many lesion combinations, fixing the problem that single lesions misattribute under redundancy. [Keinan, Sandbank, Hilgetag, Meilijson, Ruppin 2004 "Fair attribution of functional contribution in artificial and biological networks", Neural Computation](https://doi.org/10.1162/0899766041336387); [Keinan et al. 2006 "Axiomatic scalable neurocontroller analysis via the Shapley value", Artificial Life](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl.2006.12.3.333); [Aharonov et al. 2003, Neural Computation](https://doi.org/10.1162/08997660360581949) (VERIFIED existence and metadata via Crossref; REPORTED content from titles and prior knowledge, abstracts/full text NOT read).
- Information-theoretic measure: transfer entropy measures directed information flow between time series. [Schreiber 2000, Phys. Rev. Lett. 85, 461 "Measuring Information Transfer"](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.85.461) (VERIFIED metadata; content not read). Transfer entropy is not causal by itself (confounded by common drivers), so it needs intervention to back it.
- Microprocessor analogy: popular neuroscience analyses (lesions, tuning curves, correlations, dimensionality reduction) applied to a known processor recovered little of the known mechanism. [Jonas and Kording 2017, PLoS Comput Biol 13(1)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005268) (VERIFIED metadata; content REPORTED from memory of the paper, text not re-read here).
- Connectome is not function: wiring alone does not specify behaviour; neuromodulation, cellular properties, and state matter. [Bargmann and Marder 2013, Nat Methods "From the connectome to brain function"](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.2451) (VERIFIED metadata; content REPORTED from memory, not re-read).
- Degeneracy: similar network output arises from disparate parameter sets, so matching output does not identify mechanism. [Prinz, Bucher, Marder 2004, Nat Neurosci "Similar network activity from disparate circuit parameters"](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn1352); [Marder and Taylor 2011, Nat Neurosci "Multiple models to capture the variability in biological neurons and networks"](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.2735) (VERIFIED metadata; content REPORTED from memory).
- Shiu et al. 2024 own stated limits: model is a simple leaky integrate-and-fire network on connectivity plus predicted neurotransmitter; it ignores morphology and receptor dynamics, gap junctions, non-spiking neurons, internal state and long-range neuropeptides; baseline firing 0; accuracy limited by synapse and transmitter prediction; neuromodulation may explain wrong predictions; one free parameter (W_syn, size of the membrane change per synapse); some predictions validated by optogenetics. [Shiu et al., Nature 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07763-9) (read: full text via Europe PMC PMC11446845, Limitations paragraphs; VERIFIED our reading). Note: this is the FlyWire-based model; our network is MaleCNS and ALIF, so Shiu does not validate our network (INFERRED).
- Task-trained connectome models differ from wiring-only models: Lappalainen 2024 fit parameters (task-trained, connectome-constrained) for the visual system, so predictions depend on training, not wiring alone. [Lappalainen et al., Nature 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07939-3) (VERIFIED metadata; "task-trained" characterisation REPORTED via Carboncopies page, which says the Lappalainen model is a task-trained artificial visual system; not confirmed from the paper text by us).
- Behavioural fidelity without biological fidelity: worm connectome + DRL-trained interface produced realistic fly walking, called "biologically meaningless" by the authors; they urge components and interfaces grounded in biology. [Brunton et al. 2026 bioRxiv](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.20.713233) (read: abstract; REPORTED).
- Random-network baseline proposed by critics of the Eon demo (Cowley: test whether a randomly connected connectome gives the same behaviours; Turaga: random networks may walk the body); Brunton: DRL optimises behaviour so it can imitate fly behaviour with unrealistic models. [The Transmitter 2 Apr 2026](https://www.thetransmitter.org/systems-neuroscience/digital-sphinx-raises-questions-about-connectome-models/) (read: article; REPORTED).
- "Puppet effect", single-correct-action environments, outputs-only validation criticised. [Carboncopies](https://carboncopies.org/Blog/Posts/FruitFlyNotUploaded/Post/) (read: page; REPORTED, advocacy).
- Necessity/sufficiency logic misapplied in neuroscience (lesion or activation results over-read as "the circuit for X"). [Yoshihara and Yoshihara 2018, J Neurogenet](https://doi.org/10.1080/01677063.2018.1468443) (VERIFIED metadata from title; content not read).
- Test-set leakage and train/test confounds as a systematic source of irreproducible ML results. [Kapoor and Narayanan 2023, Patterns](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patter.2023.100804) (VERIFIED metadata; content REPORTED from memory).
- Behaviour should be measured in rich natural context, not only reduced tasks. [Krakauer et al. 2017, Neuron "Neuroscience Needs Behavior"](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2016.12.041) (VERIFIED metadata).
- Our own history (local, REPORTED by files, VERIFIED where we ran them): shuffle without strength matching 3/40 vs data 38/40 turned into 10/10 when strength-preserving; sides swapped 0/40; no image 0/40; P-controller 40/40; delay 50 ms 17/40; grip: network 90/135, classical 93, reflex 80, strength-matched shuffles median 82 (range 4-102). Reading: lateralization and strength explain the vision result; grip shows no role of specific connections. NOT VERIFIED: any claim that topology matters.

### Inferences
**Control battery (HYPOTHESIS), grouped by what each distinguishes; all arms run on identical seeds, frozen interfaces, held-out scenarios, fresh seeds declared in a preregistration file before the run, analysis script hashed, blinded arm labels (the analyst sees "arm 1..k"):**
1. Silence/lesion the fly part (outputs forced to 0, or to their time-average): success must fall to the no-network floor. If success survives, the designed part decides (T7).
2. Replace fly output with null signals driving the designed layer: (a) white noise at matched rate and per-channel statistics, (b) replay of a recorded fly output from a different trial (breaks the closed loop but preserves marginals), (c) time-shuffled own output. Replay isolates whether the instantaneous fly signal matters; a designed layer with state will still perform under replay if it carries the goal.
3. Wiring-null family for the fly network (distinguishes data topology from generic structure), in increasing strictness: random sparse network of equal size; degree-preserving; strength-per-neuron-preserving (in and out sum), our equal_weight shuffle; cell-type-block-preserving (edges randomised only within type-pair blocks, keeps lateralization and class structure); side swap. Interpretation: if block-preserving shuffles equal data, the claim is "type-level structure/lateralization", not "specific connections" (this is where we are, per V5 red team). Only a result where data beats block-preserving shuffles supports synapse-level specificity.
4. Trivial relay baseline: always include the hand-built minimal circuit (our left to left DNa02 20/20, sign(gL-gR) 20/20). If the full fly network is not better than the relay on a metric that the relay cannot gain (e.g. selection among two targets, distractor rejection, delay robustness, grip modulation), the network adds nothing beyond relay; also state this beforehand as the pass criterion.
5. Capacity-matched random or trained-in-sim network in place of the fly network, with the same interface: (a) random fixed, (b) same-size recurrent SNN trained by DRL/BPTT on the same task. If (b) matches or exceeds the fly network with the same interface, the fly wiring is not the source of competence (this is the digital-sphinx test applied to us).
6. Designed layer: freeze vs retrain. Report performance with D frozen from a dev set (translation reading) and with D retrained for each fly-network variant (the retrained shuffles/randoms catching up shows D, not the fly net, carries the function; earlier local report predicted "retrained shuffles may catch up", INFERRED).
7. Decision-point counting: list every location where the pipeline contains a threshold, argmax, if/else, state latch, timer or learned weight; classify as safety, translation or decision under T1-T8; report the count and the fraction of commands issued per source (today 8.8 % grip / 16.0 % base per local report, REPORTED). A decision count of zero in designed code, apart from safety, is the target.
8. Variance partition: regress action (e.g. base-pan velocity, grip force) on fly-state features and designed-state features; report unique and shared explained variance (commonality analysis), and Shapley attribution over the set {fly net, designed layer, encoder, scripted logic} using the multi-perturbation scheme above. HYPOTHESIS: report together with a lesion-based estimate, because regression is correlational.
9. Information measures: mutual information between task variable (target side, distractor present) and (a) fly output, (b) designed-layer internal state; transfer entropy fly output to designed state vs reverse; validate directionality by intervention (control 2). Caveat: estimators are biased with small trial counts (INFERRED; we have 40 trials per arm, so confidence intervals must be reported).
10. Held-out scenarios and perturbations never used for tuning: distractor of other colour on opposite side (previous RT: 0/12 with the green distractor, showing no target selection), distance beyond the dev range (dead zone: rho 0.30 m 0/10), delay, tolerance sweep (6 deg 40/40 vs 4 deg 29/40). Record the tuning scenarios in a file before testing.
11. Blind-to-side and blind-to-image controls with a correctly powered design: our H3/H4 could not discriminate by construction (|az0| >= 10 deg > 5 deg tolerance); redo with tolerance below the initial offset.
12. Preregistration items: primary metric, success tolerance, pass thresholds per arm, seeds, exclusion rules, statistical test (Wilson intervals, McNemar for paired arms, Bonferroni across arms), stated failure outcomes ("if block-preserving shuffle >= data, claim downgraded to lateralization").
**Typical errors (sourced where marked, otherwise INFERRED):**
- Task success read as biological fidelity (Brunton 2026; Carboncopies; Turaga/Cowley quotes).
- Interface carries the intelligence: trained or hand-tuned designed layer (hidden training, "hand tuning" that fits parameters to the test scenario); with a trainable interface almost any network can be made to drive the body. INFERRED; supported by the sphinx demonstration.
- Lateralization or gain mistaken for topology (our own V5 result).
- Shuffles that do not preserve strength, or that preserve everything except the one thing that mattered; conversely shuffles that preserve too much (can leave data-equivalent function). Both directions must be reported.
- Testing on the tuning scenario, dev range chosen without checking edges (our own RT point 4), threshold set after seeing results (J0 rule v2 after v1 failed, RT point 5). General leakage literature: Kapoor and Narayanan 2023.
- Degeneracy: matching a few output statistics does not identify the mechanism (Prinz et al. 2004; Marder and Taylor 2011).
- Connectome treated as complete function: missing neuromodulation, state, gap junctions, plasticity (Bargmann and Marder 2013; Shiu 2024 limits; our own "neuromodulators have no effect").
- Lesion/activation read as "the circuit for X" without accounting for redundancy or compensation (Yoshihara and Yoshihara 2018; Keinan et al. on Shapley value).
- Analysis methods validated only on systems whose mechanism is unknown (Jonas and Kording 2017): run every control battery on a toy hybrid whose decision locus we set ourselves (planted-ground-truth test, HYPOTHESIS), e.g. one version where D decides and one where F decides, and verify the battery tells them apart.
- "Brain upload" language for a task-limited model (Eon coverage; Eon's own head of engineering partly conceded). Pitch should say: simulation, MaleCNS, ALIF, partial circuit, no plasticity, no real arm.
- Inputs: encoder injecting current into hand-picked neuron classes is artificial stimulation (owner's 12 Nov ruling), and for a single scalar input all wirings that preserve the sign give the same behaviour (local report 10 Nov via 12 Nov report, REPORTED).
**Priority recommendation (INFERRED):** the cheapest informative additions, once the owner approves any build, are controls 1, 3 (block-preserving), 4, 5(b) and 10 with preregistered seeds; control 8 and 9 are expensive and weaker. A planted-ground-truth test on a toy is the check that the battery itself is valid.
